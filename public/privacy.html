Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Juicy Player is a strictly local music player. We respect and protect your privacy. This policy is designed to explain our data processing practices in the clearest and most concise way possible.

1. Information We Collect

Juicy Player does not collect, upload, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or network data.

To enable core playback functionality, the app processes only the following information locally on your device:

  • Basic audio file metadata (e.g., song title, artist, album, artwork, and duration).
  • Local playback history and custom playlists (stored exclusively on your device).

All data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any external servers.

2. System Permissions

This app requests only the essential permissions listed below, which remain entirely under your control:

  • Storage / Media Access: Required solely to scan and play local audio files on your device. The app does not access photos, documents, or any other non-audio files.

You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's system settings. If revoked, the app will be unable to read local music, but its core functionality will otherwise remain unaffected.

3. Third-Party Services

Juicy Player does not integrate any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud services. The app operates completely offline and does not transmit any data externally.

4. Data Security and Storage

All data is kept locally on your device. Uninstalling the app will automatically and permanently delete all local playback history and playlist data. We do not perform any remote processing or backup of your data.

5. Your Rights

As a strictly local application, you have complete control over your data:

  • You can clear your playback history and playlists at any time within the app.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which will permanently delete all associated data.
  • No account registration or binding of personal information is required.

6. Policy Updates and Contact

If future feature updates ever involve changes to data collection, we will notify you in advance via an in-app notification or by updating the effective date of this policy. The current version is designed to be strictly offline, with no plans for data collection.

If you have any privacy-related questions, please contact us at: hifior@foxmail.com.
